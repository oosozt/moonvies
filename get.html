Moonvies

On your TV

For Android TV, Google TV and Mi TV Stick. Takes a few minutes, once.

  1. On the TV, open Google Play and install Downloader (the orange icon, by AFTVnews).
  2. Open Downloader. When it asks, allow it to install apps.
    If it doesn't ask: TV Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → turn on Downloader.
  3. In Downloader's address box, type
    moonvies/tv.apk
    press Go, and when it has downloaded, Install.
  4. Open Moonvies from the TV's apps. It shows Pair this TV.
  5. On your phone, sign in at moonvies, open ⬇ Downloads (top right) → 📺 TV app, and type the 6-digit code it shows on the TV.

After that the TV opens straight into your account. Updates install the same way, over the old app.
